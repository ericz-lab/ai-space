# Commercial use

ai-space is source-available under the [Sustainable Use License 1.0](LICENSE). This page is a
summary to help you decide whether you need to talk to us; the license text is what binds.

## What you may do without asking

- Run and change ai-space for personal or non-commercial use.
- Run and change ai-space for your own internal business purposes: your company's Space, on your
  machines, used by your people for your work.
- Give copies to others free of charge for non-commercial purposes, as long as each copy carries
  the license and the copyright notices, and a changed copy says it was changed.
- Write apps for ai-space and distribute or sell them. The app templates and the UI kit are under
  the MIT License for that reason ([NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).

## What needs a commercial license

- Selling ai-space or a changed version of it, alone or as part of another product.
- Offering ai-space to others as a hosted or managed service, or running it for customers.
- Distributing ai-space for a fee or for a commercial purpose, a white-label product included.
- Any other use the Sustainable Use License does not grant.
